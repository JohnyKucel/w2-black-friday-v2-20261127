How a deal earns its verdict

New Low — the lowest price a deal tracker or the store itself has shown for this product. Strong Deal — 25% or more off the usual price. Solid Deal — 15–24% off. Routine Deal — under 15% off; the saving is real but small, and the note says so plainly.

How prices were checked: each sale price and usual price was read from the store's own announcement, its Black Friday page, or a deal roundup that names the store and the price, on 27 Nov 2026. Prices move fast in sale week — always confirm the price on the store page before you pay. Store links go to the store's own search for that exact product.
